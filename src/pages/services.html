Our solutions

Two platforms we bring to every engagement.

Both are proven building blocks that we tailor to your traffic, rules and integrations rather than rebuild from scratch.

Telecom platform

Synthiq Relay

A messaging core that accepts traffic from your apps and routes it across several carriers by cost, quality and country rules. If one route degrades, Relay shifts traffic automatically and keeps the receipts consistent.

  • Least-cost and quality-based routing
  • Real-time delivery dashboards
  • Per-client rating and invoicing exports
Operations AI

Synthiq Pilot

An assistant for support and operations teams. Pilot reads alerts, tickets and runbooks, drafts the next action and waits for a person to approve it, so routine work moves faster without losing control.

  • Connectors for common ticketing and chat tools
  • Approval step on every action
  • Full audit log of prompts and outcomes
Engagement models

Choose the contract that fits the uncertainty.

Dedicated team

Best when the roadmap is long and priorities shift. You get a steady team at a fixed monthly rate and direct say over the backlog.

Fixed price

Best when scope is clear and stable. We agree on deliverables, milestones and price up front, and we carry the delivery risk.

Time & material

Best for discovery, audits or evolving products. You pay for hours used, tracked weekly against an agreed budget ceiling.
